## Section: Factory Profile
Kelikuli is a resin toy and resin craft manufacturer focused on custom figurines, blind box collectibles, Zakka ornaments, and seasonal gift crafts. Since 2005, we have supported overseas buyers with factory-direct development, sampling, production, and customization service.

## Section: What We Make
Our product range includes custom resin toys, hand-painted figurines, collectible decorations, blind box series, holiday resin crafts, fridge magnets, snow globes, piggy banks, and decorative resin gifts.

## Section: Our Custom Strength
Kelikuli works with brand owners, wholesalers, gift companies, and creative product teams to turn ideas into physical resin products. Whether you already have a finished design or only a rough concept, our team can help with product structure, sample development, color matching, hand painting, packaging, and production planning.

## Section: Quality Philosophy
Every resin product depends on stable molding, clean surface finishing, accurate color application, and careful packing. Kelikuli focuses on practical production details so that custom resin toys look good in photos, feel reliable in hand, and arrive ready for retail display.
